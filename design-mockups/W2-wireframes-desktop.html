Beesyde — desktop wireframes (primary target)

The same structure as docs/24, drawn for a 1280 px browser window. Structure only, no style. Grey striped boxes are photos and maps. The yellow notes say why each block is there. Mobile (W1) adapts from this layout, not the other way round.
