Proposal B

Departure board

The top of the page is a carbon airport board. Each city is a row with a split-flap code and its local name, plus a sample pick right on the row, so you see what you get before you click. Below, on light paper, Swap is a real boarding pass and the example shows as a numbered trip.

Mood: bold, cinematic, “I’m leaving”

Proposal C

Stamp collage

Huge type on a dotted page, with the 11 cities as ring stamps scattered across it, as if just pressed. One wide search pill (offset fuchsia shadow) does both jobs: type a city or the place that's full. The example becomes three luggage tags, and the cities close the page as one big line of type.

Mood: playful, poster, most distinctive
